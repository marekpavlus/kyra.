My Story

I’m Marek, a Software Technology Engineering student at VIA University College in Denmark.

I have a passion for problem-solving and enjoy the process of designing and implementing software solutions that are both efficient and maintainable. My goal is to create applications that not only meet user needs but also adhere to best practices in software engineering.

I’m originally from Slovakia, and I have lived in Denmark since 2022. Before starting my degree, I worked full-time in manufacturing and logistics, an experience that taught me discipline, reliability and how to work effectively in international teams.

My journey into software development began with a curiosity for how things work and a desire to create. I started learning programming through online courses and personal projects, which led me to pursue formal education in software engineering.

Today, I’m continuously expanding my skills through university and personal projects, exploring technologies such as Spring Boot, .NET and distributed systems while strengthening my knowledge of software architecture, databases and web development.

I’m currently looking for professional opportunities where I can gain experience, contribute to real-world software and continue developing as a software engineer.
